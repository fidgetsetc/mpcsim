main.html is always pulled fresh from jsDelivr and run as a blob. Every local app is rewritten on load: the files it needs are downloaded into the local database (IndexedDB) and served from there, with the app's own folder as the base URL. Tick apps below to pre-download them. Apps that are not downloaded (and apps that can't be, because they need a ?query) are embedded in an iframe from a random domain listed in the repo's domains.txt, and the Mirror Refresh app pinned to the taskbar reloads the active app from another random one.
